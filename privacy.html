Privacy Policy

How DNM collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information submitted through this website.

Last updated: June 8, 2026

1. Who We Are

DNM, also known as Drugs No More, is a student-founded non-profit organisation based in Phuket, Thailand. This policy applies to information collected through this website and related DNM forms or communications.

2. Information We Collect

  • Contact details you submit, such as your name, email address, phone number, school or organisation, and message.
  • Volunteer, partner, or support request details you choose to provide.
  • Basic technical information from website hosting logs, such as page requests, browser type, device type, and approximate time of visit.

3. How We Use Information

  • To reply to questions, support requests, volunteer interest, and partnership messages.
  • To organise DNM community, education, and awareness work.
  • To keep the website secure, fix issues, and improve usability.
  • To meet legal, safety, or safeguarding responsibilities where required.

4. Cookies and Analytics

This website does not currently use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies. If analytics, forms, or embedded third-party services are added later, this policy should be updated to explain what is collected and why.

5. Sharing Information

DNM does not sell personal information. We may share information only when needed to respond to your request, operate the website, work with trusted service providers, support safeguarding, or comply with law.

6. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for the reason it was collected, unless a longer period is required for legal, safeguarding, or record-keeping purposes.

7. Your Rights

Where applicable under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act and related laws, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, withdrawal of consent, or other available rights over your personal information. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Students and Young People

If you are under 18, avoid sending sensitive personal information through the website without support from a parent, guardian, teacher, or trusted adult. DNM is not an emergency service.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact DNM at deniel@robot.co or +66 94 736 2815.
