Drugs & Their Effects

Understanding what substances do to the body and mind is one of the most powerful tools for prevention. This page provides honest, evidence-based information — free of judgment.

Educational Content Only

This page presents factual information about substances for educational and prevention purposes. If you or someone you know is struggling with drug use, please contact us for support.

Stimulant

Methamphetamine

Yaba / Ice / Crystal Meth

Short-term Effects
  • • Increased energy and alertness
  • • Elevated heart rate and blood pressure
  • • Decreased appetite and sleep
  • • Euphoria followed by intense crash
Long-term Risks
  • • Severe cognitive impairment
  • • Psychosis and paranoia
  • • Cardiovascular damage
  • • Extreme physical deterioration
Dependency Risk Very High
Cannabis

Cannabis

Marijuana / Weed / Ganja

Short-term Effects
  • • Altered perception and mood
  • • Impaired memory and coordination
  • • Increased appetite
  • • Anxiety or paranoia (high doses)
Long-term Risks
  • • Mental health issues in youth
  • • Lung damage (if smoked)
  • • Impaired brain development
  • • Reduced motivation and academic performance
Dependency Risk Moderate
Opioid

Heroin

Smack / H / Horse

Short-term Effects
  • • Intense euphoria
  • • Slowed breathing and heart rate
  • • Pain relief
  • • Nausea and vomiting
Long-term Risks
  • • Fatal overdose risk
  • • Organ failure
  • • HIV/hepatitis risk (injection)
  • • Severe physical dependence
Dependency Risk Extreme
Plant-based

Kratom

Mitragyna speciosa / Bai Kratum

Short-term Effects
  • • Stimulant at low doses
  • • Sedative / opioid-like at high doses
  • • Pain relief and mood elevation
  • • Nausea and sweating
Long-term Risks
  • • Physical dependence
  • • Withdrawal symptoms
  • • Liver damage (heavy use)
  • • Mental health impact
Dependency Risk Moderate–High
Depressant

Alcohol

Underage & heavy use

Short-term Effects
  • • Impaired judgment and coordination
  • • Slurred speech
  • • Lowered inhibitions
  • • Risk of alcohol poisoning
Long-term Risks
  • • Liver cirrhosis
  • • Brain damage (youth especially)
  • • Addiction
  • • Increased accident risk
Dependency Risk Moderate–High
Inhalant

Inhalants

Glue / Petrol / Aerosols

Short-term Effects
  • • Brief euphoria and dizziness
  • • Hallucinations
  • • Loss of coordination
  • • Sudden Sniffing Death possible
Long-term Risks
  • • Brain and nerve damage
  • • Hearing and vision loss
  • • Liver and kidney damage
  • • Cardiac arrest
Dependency Risk Moderate
Empathogen

MDMA / Ecstasy

E / Molly / Pills

Short-term Effects
  • • Intense euphoria and empathy
  • • Elevated temperature
  • • Increased energy
  • • Dehydration risk
Long-term Risks
  • • Serotonin damage
  • • Depression and anxiety
  • • Memory and cognition problems
  • • Risk of heatstroke death
Dependency Risk Moderate
Stimulant

Cocaine

Coke / Snow / Powder

Short-term Effects
  • • Short burst of euphoria
  • • Increased heart rate
  • • Overconfidence and aggression
  • • Constricted blood vessels
Long-term Risks
  • • Heart attack and stroke
  • • Nasal tissue destruction
  • • Severe depression
  • • Strong psychological dependency
Dependency Risk High
Depressant

Benzodiazepines

Valium / Xanax / Diazepam

Short-term Effects
  • • Sedation and anxiety reduction
  • • Muscle relaxation
  • • Drowsiness and confusion
  • • Memory gaps
Long-term Risks
  • • Physical and psychological dependence
  • • Dangerous withdrawal syndrome
  • • Cognitive impairment
  • • Overdose when combined with opioids
Dependency Risk High
