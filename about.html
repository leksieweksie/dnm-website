Who We Are

About DNM

We are Phuket's community-rooted drug prevention initiative — working every day to protect young people through education, empathy, and action.

2016
Founded
Our Story

Born from community need

DNM was founded in 2016 by a group of Phuket educators, parents, and community leaders who saw a growing drug problem affecting young people across the island — and decided not to wait for someone else to act.

What began as informal school talks in Kathu quickly grew into a structured prevention program operating across all three of Phuket's districts. Today DNM works with over 30 partner schools, community health centres, and local government bodies.

We believe prevention is more powerful — and more humane — than punishment. Our work is grounded in evidence, driven by compassion, and accountable to the communities we serve.

Our Journey

2016

DNM Founded

First prevention workshops delivered to three schools in Kathu district. Twelve founding volunteers.

2018

Island-Wide Expansion

Programs rolled out across all three districts — Mueang, Kathu, and Thalang. Official partnership with Phuket Provincial Education Office.

2020

Digital & Pandemic Response

Online education platform launched during COVID-19. Reached over 400 students digitally when in-person programs paused.

2023

1,000 Youth Milestone

DNM reached its 1,000th young person through active programs. Launched parent and family support workshops.

2025 — Today

Growing Stronger

1,200+ youth reached, 45 active school programs, and our first merchandise line to fund the mission. The work continues.

Our Values

These principles guide every decision, every program, and every interaction we have in the community.

Trust

We earn trust through transparency, consistency, and accountability — to students, families, and funders alike.

Evidence

Every program is built on data and research. We track outcomes, publish results, and continuously improve what we do.

Compassion

We approach drug use without judgment — as a health and social issue that requires empathy, not punishment.

Community

Prevention is a community effort. We work with — not for — the people of Phuket at every level of our programs.
