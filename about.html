Who We Are

About DNM

DNM is a student-founded non-profit organisation starting in Phuket with a simple goal: reduce drug use and make the community safer through education and awareness.

2026
Starting
Our Story

Starting with student action

DNM was founded in 2026 by a group of students learning at British International School Phuket.

We are still at the beginning. We have not launched large programs yet, so our current work is focused on building the website, creating education resources, and preparing ways to reach the Phuket community.

Our aim is to reduce drug use, encourage safer choices, and make Phuket a safer place for everyone.

Where We Are Now

2026

DNM Established

A four-person founding team of students began building DNM at British International School Phuket.

Now

Building the Foundation

We are creating the website, collecting reliable information, and shaping DNM's first education materials.

Next

Preparing Awareness Work

Our next step is to turn the research and resources into clear awareness content for the local community.

Coming Soon

Community Outreach

DNM plans to grow from a student project into something that can help more people across Phuket.

Goal

A Safer Phuket

The long-term goal is simple: reduce drug use and make Phuket safer through education, awareness, and community support.

Our Values

These principles guide how we are building DNM from the start.

Trust

We want DNM to be honest about what it is, what it is building, and what it still needs to improve.

Evidence

Our educational content should be based on real research and reliable sources, not fear or exaggeration.

Compassion

We approach drug use without judgment — as a health and social issue that requires empathy, not punishment.

Community

DNM is starting with one island and one community: Phuket.
