Our Mission

Building a
drug-free future
for Phuket

Founded in Phuket, DNM (Drugs No More) works alongside schools, families, and the community to prevent drug use before it even begins.

Our approach is rooted in education, empathy, and evidence, not fear. We believe that when young people are given the right information and genuine support, they make better choices.

How We Work

Three pillars that drive everything we do in Phuket communities.

Education

Evidence-based curriculum delivered in Phuket schools, giving students the knowledge and confidence to make safe choices.

Learn More

Community Outreach

Working directly with families, local leaders, and at-risk youth to build networks of support across all of Phuket's districts.

Support & Recovery

Connecting individuals and families with the resources, counselling, and peer support they need to rebuild healthy lives.
