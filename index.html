Our Mission

Building a
drug-free future
for Phuket

DNM is a non-profit organisation founded by a group of students learning at British International School Phuket. We aim to reduce drug use and make Phuket a safer place for everyone!

We are just getting started, so our current focus is building clear education resources, growing a founding team, and preparing ways to support the local community.
