# DNM (DRUGS NO MORE) — BRAND GUIDELINES

---

## SYSTEM RULE — DESIGN SOURCE OF TRUTH

All UI, design, typography, spacing, and branding decisions must strictly follow this document.
Do not introduce new styles outside of these rules.

---

# 1. Brand Overview

DNM (Drugs No More) is a prevention-focused initiative based in Phuket aimed at reducing drug use and promoting awareness, education, and healthier lifestyle choices.

The brand is:
- Clean
- Trustworthy
- Calm but firm
- Nature-driven
- Community-focused

### Core Mission
To prevent drug use through awareness, education, and positive alternatives.

---

# 2. Brand Personality

DNM should feel like:

- Safe and trustworthy
- Calm authority (not aggressive)
- Nature-inspired and grounded
- Supportive and educational
- Modern but human

### What the brand is NOT:
- Fear-based or overly intense
- Aggressive or judgmental
- Corporate and cold
- Overly stylised or flashy

---

# 3. Tone of Voice

### Writing style:
- Clear and simple
- Respectful and supportive
- Educational
- Calm authority

### Example tone:
- “Support starts with awareness.”
- “Helping communities make safer choices.”
- “Prevention through education and understanding.”

### Avoid:
- Threatening language
- Emotional manipulation
- Overly complex wording

---

# 4. Color System (Green-Based Nature Palette)

- HEX: #1F6B3A
- RGB: 31, 107, 58

## Deep Forest Green (Primary Dark)
- HEX: #0F2E1A
- RGB: 15, 46, 26

## Soft Green Accent
- HEX: #3FAE5A
- RGB: 63, 174, 90

## Light Background Green
- HEX: #E8F5EC

## Neutral Colors
- White: #FFFFFF
- Soft Gray: #A7B3AA
- Dark Text: #102418

---

# 5. Typography System

## Primary Font (UI / Body)
Roboto  
- Clean, readable, modern
- Used for all body text and UI

## Display Font (Headings)
Bernoru (Canva font)
- Used for titles and hero text
- Adds identity and strength

---

# 6. Logo System

## Primary Logo
Circular emblem:
- “DNM” bold center mark
- Leaf symbol integrated into typography
- “DRUGS NO MORE” descriptor underneath

Represents:
- Protection
- Nature
- Growth and recovery

---

## Logo Usage Rules
- Always maintain circular proportions
- Do not distort or rotate
- Prefer green-on-dark or green-on-white versions
- Always keep clear spacing around logo
